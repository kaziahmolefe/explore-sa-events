About Us

Explore SA Events started as a small notice board in a community hall. Today it is a website that helps South Africans and visitors find local events and share them with friends.

We are run by volunteers who care about culture, community and getting outside.

We began with a single weekend market and a printed list of stall holders. Word spread, more organisers asked to be added, and the list moved online. We now feature events from music nights to heritage walks.

Every event on the site is checked by a volunteer before it is published.

How we choose events

  1. The organiser submits the event details.
  2. A volunteer checks the date, venue and price.
  3. We confirm that the event is safe and open to the public.
  4. The event is added to the site and shared with our community.

Our values

  • Local first: we back community organisers.
  • Honest information: prices and dates are checked.
  • Open to all: events for every budget.
